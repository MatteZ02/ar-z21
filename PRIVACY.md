# Privacy policy

_Throttle, by Alppirautatieharrastajat ry. Last updated: 10 November 2026._

Throttle does not collect, store or share any personal data with us or anyone else.

- **No account, no server, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking.** The app has no backend
  and does not contact the internet.
- **Local network:** the app talks only to the model railway central unit (for example a Roco
  Z21) at the IP address set in the app, over the local Wi-Fi network. It sends driving commands
  and receives the state of the trains and the track. Nothing else is sent.
- **Data on the device:** the vehicle library, loco photos and settings are stored only on your
  phone or tablet. They are deleted when you uninstall the app. They are included in your
  device's own backup if you have one enabled; we have no access to it.
- **Camera and photos:** used only when you take or choose a photo for a loco. The photo is
  resized and stored in the app on your device.
- **Files:** when you import a Roco Z21 app export, the file you choose is read on the device;
  it is not uploaded anywhere.
